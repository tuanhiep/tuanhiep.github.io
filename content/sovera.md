<section class="about-section">
<h2><span aria-hidden="true">📐</span> Decoupled 3-Layer Architecture (Inverted Pyramid)</h2>
<div class="about-grid">
<article>
<span class="book-badge">Layer I · Executive Synthesis</span>
<h3>Guardrailed AI Narrative (Optional)</h3>
<p>Decision-first synthesis placed up front for executive clarity, yet strictly subordinated to deterministic outputs. External AI commentary cannot alter computed numbers, is cryptographically bound to the snapshot cutoff and calculation digest, permits qualitative conviction only (<code>LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH</code>), and degrades cleanly when AI is disabled.</p>
</article>
<article>
<span class="book-badge">Layer II · Epistemic Gate</span>
<h3>Evidence-Bound Assertions</h3>
<p>Only directly observed evidence and formally checked structural or accounting identities advance as hard findings. Whenever supporting inputs are incomplete, stale, or conflicting, SOVERA preserves explicit <code>UNKNOWN</code> states rather than fabricating probabilities or silent pass verdicts.</p>
</article>
<article>
<span class="book-badge">Layer III · Audit Foundation</span>
<h3>Standalone Deterministic Core</h3>
<p>Offline-ready verification kernel with zero bundled third-party LLM provider SDKs. Evaluates dependency topologies, structural exit constraints, and multi-standard accounting identities locally—linking outputs to reproducible <code>SHA-256</code> input/output lineage digests for technical auditability.</p>
</article>
</div>
</section>

<section class="about-section">
<h2><span aria-hidden="true">🛡️</span> Execution Boundaries &amp; Trust Architecture</h2>
<div class="about-grid">
<article class="atlas-sov" style="border-top-width: 3px;">
<span class="book-badge">Clean-Room &amp; Modular AI</span>
<h3>Offline-Ready, Air-Gap Deployable</h3>
<p>Topology compilation, constraint evaluation, and deterministic financial verification run entirely offline on local hardware with network feeds disabled—making SOVERA deployable inside isolated/air-gapped environments. Optional external LLM adapters and public data connectors remain strictly caller-owned outside the core kernel.</p>
</article>
<article class="atlas-arch" style="border-top-width: 3px;">
<span class="book-badge">Resilient Dirty Ingestion</span>
<h3>Schema-Contracted Verification</h3>
<p>Enterprise financial filings rarely arrive as spotless spreadsheets. Schema-contracted extraction pairs explicit confidence thresholds with deterministic accounting-identity checks (Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Gross Margin invariants) and multi-gate anti-hallucination guards—including graceful degradation when cash-flow schedules are partial.</p>
</article>
<article class="atlas-sd" style="border-top-width: 3px;">
<span class="book-badge">Honest Non-Claims</span>
<h3>Audit Lineage Without Overclaims</h3>
<p>Decision dossiers expose assumptions, rejected alternatives, and failure modes transparently. Reproducible <code>SHA-256</code> digests ensure technical traceability across runs, while keeping explicit boundaries: software telemetry does not substitute for external PKI legal signatures, statutory certification, or guaranteed market outcomes.</p>
</article>
