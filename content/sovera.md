<section class="about-section">
<h2><span aria-hidden="true">📐</span> Decoupled 3-Layer Architecture (Inverted Pyramid)</h2>
<div class="about-grid">
<article>
<span class="book-badge">Layer I · Executive Synthesis</span>
<h3>Guardrailed AI Narrative (Optional)</h3>
<p>Decision-first synthesis placed up front for executive clarity, yet strictly subordinated to deterministic outputs. External AI commentary cannot alter computed numbers, is cryptographically bound to the snapshot cutoff and calculation digest, permits qualitative conviction only (<code>LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH</code>), and degrades cleanly when AI is disabled.</p>
</article>
<article>
<span class="book-badge">Layer II · Epistemic Gate</span>
<h3>Evidence-Bound Assertions</h3>
<p>Only directly observed evidence and formally checked structural or accounting identities advance as hard findings. Whenever supporting inputs are incomplete, stale, or conflicting, SOVERA preserves explicit <code>UNKNOWN</code> states rather than fabricating probabilities or silent pass verdicts.</p>
</article>
<article>
<span class="book-badge">Layer III · Audit Foundation</span>
<h3>Standalone Deterministic Core</h3>
<p>Offline-ready verification kernel with zero bundled third-party LLM provider SDKs. Evaluates dependency topologies, structural exit constraints, and multi-standard accounting identities locally—linking outputs to reproducible <code>SHA-256</code> input/output lineage digests for technical auditability.</p>
</article>
</div>
</section>
