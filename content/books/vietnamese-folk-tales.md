<section class="about-section">
<h2><span aria-hidden="true">🌾</span> Why Village Wit Outlasted Empires</h2>
<p class="about-sublede">Along the floodplains of the Red River Delta and southern canals, peasants possessed neither vast wealth nor political office. Their sharpest safeguard against greedy landlords and corrupt magistrates was unsparing folk satire — <em>trào lộng dân gian</em>.</p>
<p class="about-sublede">That is why readers from Hanoi to London smile at the man so proud of his brand-new shirt that he answers a frantic neighbor searching for a lost wedding pig by boasting about the shirt itself — or at humble Bờm refusing ox, gold, and sandalwood in exchange for a single warm handful of sticky rice.</p>
</section>
