<section class="about-section">
<h2><span aria-hidden="true">🧠</span> Why Story-Driven Tone Training Works</h2>
<p class="about-sublede">Most learners do not struggle because Vietnamese grammar is complex — Vietnamese has no verb conjugations, no grammatical gender, and no irregular plurals. Learners stall when they memorize isolated syllables with no emotional hook.</p>
<p class="about-sublede">When you hear a bragging villager answer a neighbor asking about a missing pig while showing off a brand-new shirt, your ear binds rhythm, tone, and meaning into one durable memory — reinforced by clickable dual-voice audio, <strong>🔁 Loop Line</strong> repetition, and <strong>👁️ Hide EN / Hide VI</strong> active recall.</p>
</section>
